Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026 · Applies to the school-district tools built by The EdJustice Collective LLC, including reading plan boards, graduation progress portals, and leadership dashboards.

The short version

These tools help school districts see their own information. The information belongs to the district. It stays in the district's own Google Workspace. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for anything except running the tool the district asked for.

What we collect when you sign in

When you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, your email address, and your profile picture. We use your email address for one thing: to check that you are on the district's access list and to show you the view that belongs to your role and school.

We do not ask for access to your email messages, your files, your calendar, or anything else in your Google account.

Student information

Student records are protected under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). The district is the owner and controller of all student data. Student records live inside the district's own Google Workspace and are never copied to The EdJustice Collective's systems.

Where a tool shows student information, it shows it only to district staff whose role and school have been approved by the district, and only after they sign in with a district Google account.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use student or staff information for advertising.
  • We do not share information with anyone outside the district, except a service provider the district has approved to host or run the tool.
  • We do not keep a copy of student records after a project is handed off to the district.

How long information is kept

Your sign-in record is kept only as long as the district keeps you on its access list. Student records are kept by the district under its own retention rules. The district can remove any person's access at any time by updating its access list.

Security

Access is protected by Google sign-in and a district-managed access list. Each person sees only the school and role the district has assigned. Small groups of students are hidden in summary views so that no one can be identified by a count.

Your choices

If you have questions about your information, want to see what is held about you, or want access removed, contact the district's data coordinator or write to us at the address below. We will respond within ten business days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
